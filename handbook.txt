Navachethana   School for Special Children
With the objective of providing education along with special care to "specially-abled"               
children (children with special needs) who have intellectual, physical, and emotional challenges, 
Vahini our Seva  Trust—driven by social concern—has been working to empower them. 
Believing that they are not "disabled" but "specially-abled," and aiming to instil more life and 
spirit into them, the school for special children named 'Navachethana' was started in 2006 at 
Kalidasa Road, Koppa.  
            
®
To provide these children with the opportunity to live with dignity in society just like 
common people, free education is provided by expert teachers for children aged 5 to 18 years.  
Rather than showing social sympathy toward these children, we must provide them with 
appropriate opportunities. It is the responsibility of all of us to ensure that specially-abled 
children are not considered weak, but instead made physically and mentally strong and capable. 
As per our survey there are 54 such children in our taluk. The education/training is totally free 
                                                                                  and the transport facilities is also provided by us.

Swarna Bindu:                                                                                                                                                                      
Swarna Bindu Prashana is an ancient Ayurvedic method which is described in the Kashyapa Samhita. 
In this process, purified gold ash (Swarna Bhasma) is mixed with ghee, honey, and medicinal herbs l
ike Brahmi, Vacha and Shankhapushpi, then administered to children.                                                      
Benefits:                                                                                                                                                                                 
*It increases the immune system (disease resistance) in children.                                                                   
*It provides protection against common infections.                                                                                              
*By improving memory and concentration, it helps children achieve better academic results.
*It supports healthy digestion and overall body vitality.                                                                                      
Importance of Pushya Nakshatra:                                                                                                                                                                 
Administering Swarna Bindu on the day of Pushya Nakshatra is considered as highly auspicious in 
Ayurveda. It is believed that medicines given on this day are more potent and provide long-lasting 
benefits. As it supports overall health and growth, we are providing Swarna Bindu Prashana to school 
children every month on the day of Pushya Nakshatra in our school through expert doctors from the 
Ayurvedic Hospital, Koppa.

Anatomy and Physiology Class:                       
The importance of Anatomy and Physiology 
classes for students lies in their ability to 
provide a comprehensive understanding 
of the human body's structure and function. 
These classes are essential for students to 
pursue careers in medicine, biology, health 
sciences, and other related fields. They 
equip students with the knowledge and 
skills which is needed to navigate complex 
biological systems and fostering a deeper 
appreciation for the intricacies of life. By 
studying Anatomy and Physiology the 
students gain insights into how the body 
operates, how different systems interact 
and how the body responds to various 
stimuli. This foundational understanding 
is vital for diagnosing and treating medical 
conditions, developing new healthcare 
technologies and advancing scientific 
research. 
Anatomy and Physiology are the scientific study of the structure and their functions. A single 
organism consists of billions and trillions of cells, tissues and organs. These structures include 
both microscopic and macroscopic. Cells can be defined as being the microscopic structures 
which are tested only under the electronic microscopic, whereas organs and organ systems 
are macroscopic structures which can be easily seen through our eye. To study in detail about 
the structures and functions of organisms, Anatomy and Physiology are applied. Anatomy and 
Physiology both are derived from the normal Greek language wherein anatomy is the study of 
the structure of organisms and physiology means the study of normal mechanisms and their 
interactions that function within a living system.

Baalika Shikshana                                                                                                                          
Baalika Shikshana at the high school level includes guidance about age-related 
changes, menstruation, emotional maturity, teenage behavior, and healthy 
understanding of attraction. During adolescence (usually 13–18 years), girls 
experience physical changes such as the start of menstruation, hormonal 
development etc. Schools play an important role in providing proper health 
education. So that girls can understand that menstruation is a natural biological 
process and learn about hygiene, nutrition, and self-care. High school education 
also addresses emotional maturity, helping girls to understand mood changes, 
self-identity, confidence building, and decision-making skills. Guidance about 
teenage behavior and attraction is provided in a respectful and value-based manner, 
teaching students about healthy friendships, boundaries, respect, and responsible 
choices. Life skills education encourages self-discipline, focus on studies, career 
goals, and maintaining good mental health. Through proper awareness and 
counseling support, Baalika Shikshana ensures that high school girls grow into 
informed, confident, and responsible young women.

Over the past 23 years, the Jnanavahini Education Society® has evolved from a small 
primary school into a comprehensive educational hub, now including a High School 
and a newly inaugurated P.U. College. Our vision for the next decade is to transition 
from Present place to space-constrained facility into a state-of-the-art, self-sustained 
campus that can accommodate more students. We aim to provide a holistic environment 
where "Indianness in Education" meets modern scientific advancement.                             
While our academic results are unparalleled, maintaining a 100% SSLC pass rate for 
11 years and ranking in the Taluk and our physical infrastructure has reached its limit. 
We are currently educating 680 students across Pre-Primary, Primary, High School, and
P.U.C. levels. Due to a critical shortage of space, we are unable to build the necessary 
classrooms to meet increasing demand. Existing Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and 
Computer laboratories are inadequate in space and resources to meet the needs of 
both school and college students; therefore, spacious and fully equipped labs are 
urgently required.                                                                                                                                     
Academic Expansion for Overall Infrastructure Development:                                                  
Our next 10-Year Development Plan is to ensure the future of our students; the Trust has 
outlined a comprehensive expansion project of acquiring approximately 10 acres of land: 
The institution is proposing a comprehensive Academic Expansion plan aimed at 
strengthening and modernizing its overall infrastructure to meet growing educational 
demands. This expansion will focus on the construction of advanced academic blocks, 
research centers, libraries, seminar halls, auditoriums, student amenities, hostels, 
sports facilities, and administrative buildings. It will also include the integration of 
modern technology, digital learning systems, upgraded laboratories, and sustainable 
infrastructure development.                                                                                                                
The objective of this large-scale expansion is to enhance academic excellence, promote 
research and innovation, improve student support services, and create a holistic learning 
environment aligned with national and global educational standards. The total 
estimated cost for the Academic Expansion and overall infrastructure  development is 
approximately 
₹.50 crores
This investment reflects the institution's long-term vision to 
provide world-class facilities and ensure sustainable growth for  future generations.
The Sevavahini Trust /  Jnanavahini Education Society® has proven its commitment to 
excellence by providing quality education to the Koppa region for over two decades. 
However, to take the next step, we require the support of philanthropists and funding
partners. We request to join hands with us in this nation building activities.

# Infrastructure & The 10-Year Vision

Complete transparency on where funding goes and the scale of our ambition.

## Current Infrastructure — Phase 1

The ongoing construction of our new academic block represents a **~₹2.3 Crore investment** to date. This is our skin in the game — a commitment that speaks louder than words.

Current facilities include:
- Smart Classrooms with digital learning tools
- Well-equipped Science and Computer Laboratories
- Library and Reading Room
- Sports grounds and outdoor activity areas
- Dedicated Navachethana wing for specially-abled students

## The Masterplan

Our vision is a **10-acre self-sustained Residential Campus** that will permanently anchor the region's education infrastructure. This campus will serve as a model for rural education across Karnataka and beyond.

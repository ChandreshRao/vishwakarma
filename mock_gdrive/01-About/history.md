# History of Seva Vahini Trust & Jnanavahini

The Seva Vahini Trust was established as a foundational "Mother institution" for educational and social welfare initiatives, guided by the philosophy of "Vidya Hridya Sada Bhaveth" (May knowledge always be within the heart).

## Early Years (1998–2003)
Our journey began with the Prabodhini Shishu Mandira in 1998-99. By 2002-03, it received government recognition. In May 2002, the Nachiketa Primary School was inaugurated with just 7 students.

## Growth & Infrastructure
- **2005**: Ground floor of the Kalidasa Road building inaugurated.
- **2006**: "NAVACHETHANA" school for especially abled children established.
- **2011**: English medium section "Jnana Vaahini" officially inaugurated.
- **2024**: Jnanavahini Pre-University (PU) College started.

Today, the institution accommodates over 680 students and continues to expand its mission of holistic education.

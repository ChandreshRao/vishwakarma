# Partner With Us

Provide actionable, frictionless ways for donors, HNIs, and institutions to collaborate or fund.

## The Collaborative Invitation

We extend an open call to urban institutions for **summer immersion camps, eco-labs, and cultural exchanges**. Imagine your students spending a week in the Western Ghats, learning alongside rural children — it's a transformative experience for both sides.

## Sponsorship Avenues

There are clear, impactful ways you can contribute:

- **Sponsor a Digital Lab** — Equip a classroom with modern learning tools
- **Fund a Rural School Bus** — Connect remote villages to education
- **Support Navachethana** — Help specially-abled children access free education
- **Scholarship Fund** — Sponsor a student's entire academic journey

## Transparency & Tax Benefits

All donations to the **Seva Vahini Trust** are eligible for **80G Tax Exemption**. We maintain complete financial transparency and provide detailed utilization reports to every donor.

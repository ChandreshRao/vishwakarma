# Academic & Extra-Curricular Achievements


Rural education **can** compete at the highest state levels. Here is the proof.

## Academic Excellence

- **11 Consecutive Years** of 100% SSLC Pass Rate
- **~50% Distinction Rate** consistently maintained
- Multiple students clearing competitive exams including NEET, JEE, and KCET

## Sports Triumphs

Our students have proven their mettle on the field as much as in the classroom:

- **50+ Students** awarded at the Taluk level
- **20+ Students** awarded at the District level
- **3 State-Level Champions**

From Karate to Athletics, Jnanavahini athletes represent the best of rural Karnataka's sporting talent.

## Cultural Prowess

- **State-level prizes** in Prathibha Karanji
- **District and Taluk level champions** in multiple cultural categories
- Regular representation at inter-school and inter-district competitions

## The Bigger Picture

These achievements are not just numbers — they are proof that with the right values, discipline, and opportunity, children from farming families in the Malnad region can stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the best in the state.

# Our Ecosystem

## Nachiketha Higher Primary School

**Foundation in Mother Tongue (Kannada) & Early Development**

Nachiketha Higher Primary School forms the bedrock of the Jnanavahini ecosystem. With a strong emphasis on building a solid foundation in the mother tongue, Kannada, the school nurtures young minds during their most formative years. The curriculum is designed to encourage curiosity, creativity, and a deep connection to local culture and heritage.

## Jnanavahini English Medium High School

**Competitive Excellence & Modern Learning Tools**

Transitioning students to English medium, Jnanavahini High School focuses on competitive academic excellence. Equipped with **Smart Classes** and modern pedagogical tools, the school prepares students for the challenges of higher education and beyond. The result speaks for itself: **11 consecutive years of 100% SSLC pass rates**.

## Jnanavahini Pre-University (PU) College

**Commerce & Science Departments**

The PU College offers both Commerce and Science streams, equipped with well-furnished laboratories and dedicated coaching for national-level entrance examinations including **NEET**, **JEE**, and **KCET**. The college bridges the gap between school education and professional aspirations.

## Navachethana — Special School

**"Specially-Abled, Not Disabled"**

Navachethana is the heart of our inclusive philosophy. Providing **100% free education and transport** to specially-abled children, this wing has graduated over **40 students** who have gone on to lead independent, dignified lives. At Navachethana, every child is celebrated for their unique abilities.

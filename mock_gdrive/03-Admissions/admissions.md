# Admission Procedure & Fees

## Procedure
1. **Visit School**: Prospective parents are invited to visit the campus during office hours (9:00 AM - 4:00 PM).
2. **Registration**: Fill out the online application form or collect a physical form from the school office.
3. **Interactive Session**: An informal session with the child and parents.
4. **Documentation**: Submit necessary documents (Birth Certificate, Transfer Certificate, etc.).

## 80G Tax Benefit
The Seva Vahini Trust centralizes all financial operations. Donors and parents contributing to the school's development are eligible for 80G tax benefits.
